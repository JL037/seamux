The chat window now shows what a waiting chat is blocked on, as its card does: an open question to answer, a tool call to approve, or a dialog to choose from, so you can answer it without closing the chat. While a question is open, its form takes an answer of your own in a "Type your answer here" row after its options, as Claude Code's own dialog does, and the reply box is hidden on the card and in the chat window until the question is answered.
