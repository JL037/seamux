The chat window now shows what a waiting chat is blocked on, as its card does: an open question to answer, a tool call to approve, or a dialog to choose from, so you can answer it without closing the chat. An answer of your own to a question goes in the reply box, on the card or in the chat window, in place of a box inside the question: it answers the question left without a pick, and the picks you made answer the rest.
