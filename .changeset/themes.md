Themes: a new Themes tab in settings makes and edits themes, each a light and a dark set of colours, and switches every open board to one at once. Picking a theme in the tab's list previews it, with any unsaved edits, on that browser alone until you leave the tab, and its Light and Dark tabs preview each variant. A theme can colour each column's dot on its own, with `--column-idle`, `--column-waiting`, `--column-working`, `--column-done`, `--column-pinned` and `--column-attention`. A theme can also pick a watermark, so far a pride heart, drawn by seamux in the bottom-right corner behind the board, with its stripes and opacity set per variant by `--watermark-1` to `--watermark-6` and `--watermark-opacity`. A theme needs only its two brand colours; the board works out the rest of its palette from them, and anything else it sets replaces what was worked out. Below the editor, a switch, Allow POST /theme/set, off by default: with it on, a JSON POST to `/theme/set`, `{"name": "pink-candy", "color": "light"}`, with the board's user and password, switches the theme from a script, from this Mac or over mDNS. `color` is optional, and switches every browser to light or dark until its own toggle switches it back. Debug's Clear all themes removes every saved theme.
