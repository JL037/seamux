The dispatch box takes images and files, as a chat's full view does: paste or drop them into the prompt, or pick them with the paperclip. Each shows as `[Image #1]` or `[File #1]` where it went in, and the new session gets it with its first prompt. The controls under the prompt now sit in one row: the agent, the directory and a Worktree checkbox on the left, the paperclip and Dispatch well apart on the right.
